How The Reading Room works

You'll work through four short rounds. Each one shows you something a person has said or written — a radio interview, a few text messages, an email, a chat with a teacher.

Your job is to read it carefully and tell Iris (your companion in the game) what you think is actually going on with that person.

Iris is going to push back gently if you say things the text doesn't actually show. That's the whole point of the game — we're practising paying close attention to what's really there.

The last round has a twist. Watch out for it.

You can restart at any time. There's no winning or losing — your reflection at the end is the artefact.
